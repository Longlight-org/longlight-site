Who we are

Longlight conducts research to support better decisions. Our Better Places work explores places, everyday experience, connections and cost.

What we collect

For this work, we read public records, including building permits and transit schedules, available without an account. We focus on places and patterns and do not republish personal contact details found in records.

How we access records

Our collection policy is to identify automated requests as Longlight-Intelligence research, make one request at a time with pauses, and stop on an access block, rate limit, challenge or request to pause. We do not work around those restrictions.
