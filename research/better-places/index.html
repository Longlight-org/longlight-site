Longlight Research · Better Places

Places, connections,
and cost.

How the places we build, and the connections between them, shape everyday life, what it costs, and who pays.

The places
in a day

A home. A workplace. A school, a shop, a park. The places in our lives are connected by the journeys we make between them.

We want to understand how decisions about those places shape daily life: what people can reach, how they experience the places they use, and what it takes to make them better.

What we’re
exploring

  • Places: what gets built, where, and when it becomes usable.
  • Connections: how people move between home, work, play and daily needs.
  • Cost: what those connections cost public budgets and households.

Our broader Better Places research also asks how people experience workplaces and shared spaces, and what evidence shows they are getting better.

This is a research program in development, not a set of findings.
